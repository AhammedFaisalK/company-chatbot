# Aurora Desk Co. — Company Knowledge Base

## About Us

Aurora Desk Co. was founded in 2021 by two remote-work engineers who were
frustrated with flimsy standing desks. We design and sell ergonomic desks,
chairs, and accessories for remote workers and small businesses. We are
based in Rivermont, Meridia, and ship nationwide within Meridia.

## Our Mission

To make ergonomic, high-quality home-office furniture accessible and
affordable for remote workers everywhere.

## Bulk / Corporate Orders

Orders of 10 or more items qualify for a 15% discount and custom invoicing.
Contact support@auroradesk.example with "Bulk Order" in the subject line to
request a quote. Bulk orders typically ship within 10–14 business days.

## Shipping Policy

- Free standard shipping within Meridia on orders over $150.
- Orders under $150 have a flat shipping fee of $12.
- Standard delivery: 3–7 business days.
- Express delivery: 2 business days, additional $25.
- We do not currently ship outside Meridia.
- Tracking information is emailed once the order ships.

## Refund & Return Policy

- We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee from the delivery date.
- Items must be returned in original condition with original packaging
  where possible.
- Return shipping is free — we email a prepaid shipping label.
- Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the
  returned item, back to the original payment method.
- Assembled furniture can still be returned; disassembly instructions are
  included in the return confirmation email.

## Cancellation Policy

- Orders can be canceled for a full refund at any time before they ship.
- Once an order has shipped, it falls under the standard 30-day return
  policy instead of cancellation.
- To cancel, contact support with your order number as soon as possible.

## Warranty

- Desk frames (Glide and Mini): 5-year warranty against structural defects.
- Glide Desk motor/electronics: 2-year warranty.
- Ergo Chair: 2-year warranty.
- Chair Lite: 1-year warranty.
- Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, unauthorized modification,
  or normal wear and tear (e.g., fabric fading).
- To make a warranty claim, email support with your order number, a
  description of the issue, and photos if possible.
